In this tutorial, I will guide you through creating music online, using one of the easiest, free and best tools available!

The program we are going to use is BeepBox by John Nesky.

To continue with this tutorial, you can search for BeepBox in your browser, or simply click one of the buttons below:

When you are on the site, you can already start creating some music, but first I will guide you through some of its features to help you learn how everything works.

1. First, you can see "Type" at the bottom left, under "Volume". It is a window that lets you choose from various instruments. Click on the "Type" window and choose the instrument you want to use.

2. After you have done that, you can set the volume of your instrument.

3. Then you can set the rhythm. I suggest using "Freehand", as it lets you choose the length of each note freely, so you can make every note as long or as short as you want.

4. Now you can set the tempo you want. The tempo is basically how fast the song plays.

5. Now there is the Key. You can choose the key you want. I suggest leaving the default setting, but for some songs you may want to change it.

6. Now you can choose the Scale, which basically determines how many boxes where you can place notes you have. I suggest using Expert.

7. Now, in Preferences, you will find a couple of options. These are the options I suggest enabling. The ones marked with ✓ are the ones I recommend having enabled.


1. Auto Play on Load [ X ]

2. Automatically View Current Bar [✓]

3. Hear Previw of Added Notes [✓]

4. Show Piano Keys [✓]

5. Highlight "Fifth" of Song Key [ X ]

6. Allow Adding Notes Not In Scale [ X ]

7. Use Current Scale As Default [✓]

8. Show Notes From All Channels [✓]

9. Show octave Scrollbar [✓]

10. Customize All Instruments [ X ]

11. Instrument Copy/Paste Buttons [✓]

12. Enable Channel Muting [✓]

13. Display Song Data In Url [✓]

14. Choose Layout | You can choose the layout you like the most.

15. Light Theme | You can decide whether you want to enable it or not; it depends on your preferences.

16. In note recording setup, check the ✓ on the first option (Add a Record button next to the [>] Play button). This way, when you click the button, it will start a 3-second countdown, and then you can create music using your keyboard.

In "Edit", you can see a bunch of shortcuts for doing various things. Now that we are ready, you can start by clicking on the boxes to place some notes.

1. Once you have placed some notes, you can hear the whole thing you have created by clicking [>] Once you click it, it will play all the notes you have placed in the boxes.

2. When you have filled the whole space, like on the first page, you can click on the next page and click the arrows pointing upward. This will add the second page.

3. After that, I suggest going to the purple bar at the bottom ma. Click in the middle at the end of the bar, and drag it to the end, or to wherever you want your song to finish. This will make the program loop the song during playback and determine where the music ends when exported.

4. Once you are done with your music, you can go to "File" and choose one of the available options.

5. + New Blank Song will erase everything and let you create music from the beginning.

6. Import Song will let you import a song that you have previously exported and continue working on it from where you left off.

7. Export Song will let you export the music to your PC. You can choose which format you want to export it in.

8. JSON, MID, and MIDI are the formats you should choose if you want to continue working on your music in the future. You can import these files again later. The other formats cannot be imported back into BeepBox.

9. If you want to publish your music and you are happy with how it sounds, I suggest choosing .MP3 or .WAV.

10. After that, you can choose how many times you want the program to loop your song. You can also check the Outro and Intro options if you want. They are usually not necessary, but you might want to use them depending on your song.

11. Once you have done everything, click Export and wait for the download to finish. That's pretty much it! I hope this tutorial helped you get started with BeepBox.
