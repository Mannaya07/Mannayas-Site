Hellow All!

In this tutorial, I will help you download League of Legends.

Downloading League of Legends is very simple, and it will not take too long, depending on how fast your internet connection is.



Minimum requirements



First of all, you need at least the minimum requirements: 30gb of free space, Operating System: Windows 10, Architecture = x64, Processor: Intel Core i3-530/AMD A6-3650, 4GB RAM, DirectX 10, Nvidia GeForce 9600 GT/AMD Radeon HD 6570, Intel HD Graphics 4600, Resolution = 1024x768.

If you have at least the minimum requirement to play League of Legends, we can start with our download.



Downloading League of Legends



To start downloading it, we first have to go to the official site of League of Legends. I left you a button below that will automatically take you to the official site where you can download the game, or you can search in your browser for "League of Legends download".

When you are on the page, click Download and a download should start. The download is the Client, because it will first download the Riot Client, and after you have installed it, you can then go install League of Legends from the client.



Creating an Account and Downloading League of Legends



Once the download has finished, click on the file that you downloaded "make sure that it's from the official site where you downloaded the file". Click on it, and another download should start. Wait until it has finished, and then the Riot Client will start. Once it has started, it will tell you if you have an account and want to log in, or if you don't have one and want to create one. If you already have one, put in your credentials and you will be able to log in. If you are having trouble, you can click "I don't remember my password" and then follow what it tells you to do. If you want to create an account, you will have to set a Name, an Email, and a Password. Make sure to set up a good password with Symbols, Uppercase letters, Numbers, and nothing referring to you, to make the password as safe as possible. After you have logged in or created an account, you will be able to access the Riot Client and install League of Legends. In the client, you can install more games, like Valorant, Legends of Runeterra, etc..., not only League of Legends. But since you are interested in League of Legends, once you are in, search for League of Legends and click Download. Once it has downloaded, click Start, and in-game there will be a final download that is usually faster than the other ones. After that, you will be able to fully start playing League of Legends.
