What is the Pomodoro Technique?



The Pomodoro Technique is a technique that helps your concentration and Mental Strength to continue studying. Other than that, it helps you remember more things thanks to how it works, and it can even help you like the Subject/etc... you are studying, if it's something that you don't like.



How the Pomodoro Technique works?



The original Pomodoro Technique works by deciding the assignment we want to do and setting up a 25-minute timer. After 25 minutes have passed, we take a 5-10 minute break, and then we start again and again until 4 Pomodoros are completed. Then, we take a longer break, a 20-30 minute break. After that break, we start the cycle again and again, until we are happy with what we studied or did.

One of the goals of the Technique is to help reduce the effect of internal and external interruptions. Since a Pomodoro is indivisible, when you are interrupted during a Pomodoro, either the other activity must be noted and deferred, or the Pomodoro must be abandoned.



How to start!



To start, it's very simple. "Even if sometimes there can be some distractions, that's normal, we are human." You have to decide on a subject or a task you want to do. Once you've done that, remove all the distractions that are around you, set up a 25-minute timer, and start studying. During the 25 minutes, try to focus only on the task you are doing. Try not to go on social media, check your phone, etc...

For example, if I have to study Math, I can decide to study the assignment that my teacher gave me. I set up a 25-minute timer and start studying. When the timer has ended, I take a 5-minute break, and then I start another Pomodoro.



Pomodoro 1: 25min Study -> 5 minutes break

Pomodoro 2: 25min Study -> 5 minutes break

Pomodoro 3: 25min Study -> 5 minutes break

Pomodoro 4: 25min Study -> 20-30 minutes break
