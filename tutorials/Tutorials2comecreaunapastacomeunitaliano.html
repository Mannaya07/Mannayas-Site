Hellow ALL!

In this tutorial, you will find out how to make pasta like a real Italian.


First, choose the type of pasta you like. There are (PENNE, CHIOCCIOLE, SPAGHETTI, RICCIOLI, ETC...)

After you choose the type of pasta you like the most, you have to choose your favorite brand of pasta and buy it. "If you want a tutorial on how to make pasta yourself, there will be one in the future".


Once you have everything, take a stockpot and fill it with about 80% water. Add some salt and wait until the water boils.

When the water boils, add the pasta. Every once in a while, taste the pasta to see if it is ready.

A few minutes before the pasta is ready, you can prepare some tomato sauce and heat it in a saucepan.

When the pasta is ready, put the sauce in a plate, add some grated cheese to the sauce, and then put the pasta on the plate.

Toss the pasta in the sauce and add some more grated cheese.


Now that everything is done, enjoy your pasta and Buon Appetito!
